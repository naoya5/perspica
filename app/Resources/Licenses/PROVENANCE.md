# Legal-text provenance

Retrieved with FetchUrl on **2026-11-13**. Legal wording is reproduced without
translation or editorial rewriting. FetchUrl returns a text/Markdown rendering,
not the original HTTP response bytes; webpage formatting and final newlines can
differ from the publisher's HTML. The local SHA-256 ledger is
`license-text-sha256.json`. It identifies these copies, not the downloaded model
or a native binary.

frameworks. The Python PDF worker (pdf2zh-next, BabelDOC, PyMuPDF and their
dependencies) and the fonts, CMaps and models it downloaded are no longer used,
installed or distributed, so their license texts were removed from this folder.
