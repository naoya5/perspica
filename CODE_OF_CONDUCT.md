# Code of Conduct

Perspica follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) and when you
represent the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember that many people here write in their
  second language.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Do not harass, insult or demean anyone, and do not post sexual or violent content.
- Do not publish anyone's private information, including translated text, documents or screen
  pictures that are not yours to share.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/naoya5/perspica/security/advisories/new),
starting the title with "Code of Conduct". You can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer reviews every report and may remove content, warn, or ban a person from the
project, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
